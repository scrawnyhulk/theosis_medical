Nutritional Nerd Out

Mechanisms. How stuff actually works. Pick a topic — no need to scroll for days. Compulsory if you are me.

Herein lies a rabbit hole of nutritional and physiologic nerditude. The “why” behind so many things related to our health and how to navigate through it all.

Why won’t I shut up about protein? What do I mean by energy? What actually causes type 2 diabetes, and can I reverse it?

How do we grow muscle? Don’t care about growing muscle? Why you should care about growing muscle. (No, I’m not saying you have to be jacked and tan.) Muscle serves countless beneficial purposes, and we should at least get it some flowers and let it know we care every once in a while.

But seriously, I love this stuff. You don’t have to, but I think you’ll be happy you stuck around by the end of it.
