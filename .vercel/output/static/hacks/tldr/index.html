TL;DR

If someone asks what to actually do, send them this. Do these most days. The numbered cards are the why, the exceptions, and the recipes.

This is the 20%. Not a 12-week program. Not a personality. The short list I actually use on a work week.

  1. 01Why Eating Healthy Feels So HardThis is biology, not a character flaw. Carb plus fat together hijacks the brain. Know that first.Full hack
  2. 02Where to StartPick your calorie target per day based on your goals.Full hack

    The goal

    The 10× walkthrough in the video. 10, 11, or 12. Using 10 because the math is clean. Lose too fast? Bump to 11 or 12.

    Calories / day
    1800

    10 × body weight (× 10–12 in the video)

    Protein
    180 g

    1 g per pound

    Leftover after protein
    1080 cal

    720 cal from protein at 4 cal/g. Real meat brings some fat with it.

  3. 03Don't Drink CaloriesWater, coffee, tea, diet soda. This is low-hanging fruit and can easily chisel off thousands of calories per week from your current diet.Full hack
  4. 04One Gram Per PoundAbout 1 gram of protein per pound — goal weight is fine if you have a lot to lose.Full hack
  5. 05The Protein × 10 TrickLook at the nutrition label. Add a zero to the protein grams. If that number is the same as or higher than the calories, it is 40% or more protein — a great fat-loss food.Full hack
  6. 06Fast Food HacksExtra meat, no bun, skip the fries, diet drink.Full hack
  7. 07Regular Food HacksGreek yogurt, tuna, chicken, egg whites, Fairlife, Legendary pastries — and anything else that hits the protein × 10 trick. If it passes that test, it works.Full hack
  8. 08Exercise HacksOne set to failure with rest-pause: a push, a pull, and a leg movement, 2–4 times per week.Full hack
  9. 09Intermittent Fasting HacksNarrow your eating window and skip a meal or two. Skip breakfast daily, eat one meal a day, or skip an entire day of eating once or twice a week. All of them decrease the amount of fuel going in so you can burn more going out.Full hack
