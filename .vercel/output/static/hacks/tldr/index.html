TL;DR

This is the 20% that gives 80% of the results. Do this 80% of the time.

If someone needs a quick reference on how to approach a healthier lifestyle in a hectic life, give them this and they can delve in further later if they want.

  1. 01Why Eating Healthy Feels So HardThis is biology, not a character flaw. Our brain thinks it's 10,000 BC and we will starve tomorrow. Carbs and fats eaten together hijacks the brain and sends a drug-like reward signal that makes it very hard to stop eating as our body sees it as fuel that we may not see again for a while.Full hack
  2. 02Where to StartPick your calorie target per day based on your goals.Full hack

    Calories = body weight × a goal number. Protein = 1 g per pound.

    The goal

    At this goal, calories are body weight × 10–12. Shown is 10. Too fast? Increase to 11 or 12.

    On insulin or other glucose-lowering meds? Don’t start a drastic calorie cut without talking with your physician first.

    Enter a weight to see the targets.

  3. 03Don't Drink CaloriesWater, coffee, tea, diet soda. This is low-hanging fruit and can easily chisel off thousands of calories per week from your current diet.Full hack
  4. 04One Gram Per PoundEat about 1 gram of protein per pound of body weight — goal weight is fine if you have a lot to lose. Individuals over 60 years old may need even more.Full hack
  5. 05The Protein × 10 TrickLook at the nutrition label. Add a zero to the protein grams. If that number is the same as or higher than the calories, it is 40% or more protein — a great fat-loss food.Full hack
  6. 06Fast Food HacksExtra meat, no bun, skip the fries, diet drink.Full hack
  7. 07Regular Food HacksGreek yogurt, tuna, chicken, steak, egg whites, Fairlife milk, Legendary pastries — and anything else that hits the protein × 10 trick. If it passes that test, it works.Full hack
  8. 08Exercise HacksOne set to failure with rest-pause: a push, a pull, and a leg movement, 2–4 times per week.Full hack
  9. 09Intermittent Fasting HacksNarrow your eating window and skip a meal or two. Skip breakfast daily, eat one meal a day, or skip an entire day of eating once or twice a week. All of them decrease the amount of fuel going in so you can burn more going out.Full hack
